## What’s Working Well

- The Home portrait remains prominent on desktop and now fits nearly fully in the mobile opening view.
- Mobile contact links are now readable at 14.4px, and the menu remains keyboard-operable.
- Experience cards and Contact maintain the same visual language as Home.

## Issues Found
### Issue 1: Desktop contact links are small
- **What**: Contact link text renders at about 11px on desktop.
- **Where**: Contact page, email and profile link rows.
- **Why it matters**: The key contact actions are less prominent and take more effort to scan than the surrounding copy.
- **Suggested fix**: Increase desktop contact-link text to at least 12–13px while retaining the mobile sizing.

## Priority Fixes for Next Attempt
1. Slightly enlarge desktop contact-link text for easier scanning.
